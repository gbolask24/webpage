# Strategy

## What the site is for

1. Give a recruiter or hiring manager a quick, accurate picture of what Gbolagade builds, with a
   case study per system and links to the public repos.
2. Rank for searches such as "AI Engineer", "AI Automation Architect", "AI engineer London",
   "agentic engineering" and "RAG".
3. Take consult enquiries from prospective clients.

## Standing content rules

- No employer names. Describe roles and systems by function and industry.
- No numeric impact metrics on the site.
- No em dashes in copy. No mention of SEO or digital marketing work.
- British English, first person, plain wording. Say the specific thing.
